## 1. Executive Summary & Problem Statement

Reconciliation mismatches between internal enterprise purchase ledgers and supplier-reported tax filings (GSTR-2B) represent an existential compliance and working capital challenge for Indian enterprises:
- Under **Section 16(2)(aa) of the CGST Act**, recipients cannot legally claim ITC unless the supplier has furnished invoice details in GSTR-1 and communicated them in auto-generated GSTR-2B.
- Under **Rule 37A (CGST Rules, 2017)**, buyers must reverse claimed ITC in Table 4(B)(2) of GSTR-3B if their supplier fails to file GSTR-3B by **November 30** following the end of the financial year.
- If reversed past the November 30 cutoff, **mandatory interest at 18% p.a. accrues under Section 50(1)** from the date of the initial claim.
- Under **Rule 48(4)**, B2B invoices generated by vendors exceeding statutory turnover thresholds (₹5 Cr) are legally void without a valid 64-character Invoice Reference Number (IRN) and signed QR code.

### The Month-End AP Reconciliation Dilemma
When finance teams reconcile 200 purchase records against 170 GSTR-2B entries, they discover **30 missing invoices** representing substantial tax exposure. Finance teams traditionally face a costly lose-lose trade-off:
1. **The Blanket Chase Approach:** Accounts Payable harasses all 30 vendors indiscriminately, eroding commercial trust and wasting hundreds of hours on vendors who simply file late every quarter.
2. **The Ostrich Approach:** Waiting until the November 30 statutory deadline, only to discover genuine vendor defaults when all payment retention leverage is gone, forcing catastrophic cash reversals and 18% interest penalties.

### The Solution: Deterministic 3-Signal Action Triage
The **GST Credit-at-Risk Forecaster** eliminates this dilemma. Rather than presenting a flat discrepancy list, our deterministic decision engine fuses **IRN portal evidence**, **commercial AP retention leverage**, and **6-month filing periodicity** into three actionable operational buckets:

- 🟢 **Safe to Hold (e.g., 25 invoices / ₹1.4L tax):** Timing differences with high commercial AP leverage (unpaid balance exceeds tax risk) and verified e-invoice footprints. Retain supplier payments until filing; do not harass the vendor.
- 🟡 **Watch (e.g., 4 invoices / ₹32k tax):** Moderate exposure with upcoming statutory deadlines. Monitor in the next filing cycle and trigger automated compliance reminders.
- 🔴 **Chase Now (e.g., 1 invoice / ₹2.4k tax):** Zero AP leverage, missing mandatory IRN, and a sudden break in filing history. Immediate commercial escalation and CFO-level intervention required.

## 4. 3-Signal Action Triage Engine & 4-State IRN Modeling

### The 4-State IRN Engine & Score Mutation

A missing IRN should not automatically be treated as a compliance failure. Applicability depends on the supplier's turnover band (< ₹5 Cr vs > ₹5 Cr), transaction type, and statutory exemptions. The pipeline implements a 4-state IRN model:

| IRN State | Regulatory Meaning | Effect on Score | Triage Rationale |
|:---|:---|:---:|:---|
| **`PRESENT`** | Authenticated on the IRP portal | **+20** | High confidence: invoice is officially logged in the government database. |
| **`ABSENT_APPLIES`** | Required to generate IRN, but failed to do so | **-15** | Genuine non-compliance under Rule 48(4); raises chase urgency. |
| **`ABSENT_NOT_APPLICABLE`** | Vendor turnover < ₹5 Cr (exempt) | **0** | Neutral: legitimate small supplier operating legally without an IRN. |
| **`ABSENT_UNKNOWN`** | External portal timeout, captcha, or unverified | **0** | Neutral: zero-penalty fallback; never guess into penalty without proof. |

```
[Invoice with Absent IRN]
          │
          ▼
[TinyFish WF3 (Rule Check) or WF4 (Portal Query)]
          │
          ▼
    irn_state (Enum)
    ├── PRESENT (+20 pts)
    ├── ABSENT_APPLIES (-15 pts)
    ├── ABSENT_NOT_APPLICABLE (0 pts)
    └── ABSENT_UNKNOWN (0 pts)
          │
          ▼
calculateRoutingScore()

          │
          ▼

```

## 5. TinyFish Web Agent Integration

[TinyFish](https://tinyfish.ai) is an autonomous web exploration and content extraction agent architecture. In this showcase application, TinyFish workflows operate in **Sandboxed Multi-Agent Simulation Mode** (with cached official CBIC/GSTN statutory schemas) to eliminate portal IP blocks and CAPTCHA rate-limiting while demonstrating how real-time regulatory web agents bridge static enterprise data with 4-state IRN verification.

### The 4 Production Workflows

| ID | Workflow Name | Target Endpoint | Description | Scoring Engine Impact |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **WF1** | **Deadline Extension Tracker** | `GET /api/tinyfish/workflow-1/deadline-extension` | Evaluates CBIC notifications (Notification 12/2024 schema) to track whether Section 16(4) reversal deadlines have been extended. | Surfaces active Nov 30 statutory horizon on dashboard header banner. |
| **WF2** | **Vendor Registry Verifier** | `GET /api/tinyfish/workflow-2/vendor-status` | Evaluates simulated taxpayer registry schema (`services.gst.gov.in`) for active/cancelled status and return filing track. | Provides qualitative audit proof before vendor escalation. |
| **WF3** | **IRN Applicability Checker** | `GET /api/tinyfish/workflow-3/irn-applicability` | Evaluates CBIC Notification No. 10/2023 r/w Rule 48(4) against supplier turnover band (< ₹5 Cr vs > ₹5 Cr) and transaction type. | Upgrades absent IRN from default penalty (`-15`) to neutral (`0`) when supplier is sub-threshold (< ₹5 Cr). |
| **WF4** | **Vendor IRN Portal Lookup** | `GET /api/tinyfish/workflow-4/vendor-lookup` | Queries simulated IRP registry schema (`einvoice1.gst.gov.in`) to check whether vendor GSTIN is e-invoice mandated. | Feeds the 4-state IRN engine with verified status; supports fallback defense mode. |

### Offline Resilience & Zero-Penalty Defense
- **SQLite Caching (24h TTL):** Every lookup is cached with SHA-256 keys in `backend/data/tinyfish_cache.db`.
- **Deterministic Parity:** When offline, the app serves structurally identical responses with `CACHE_HIT` / `OFFLINE_FALLBACK` badges.
- **Zero-Penalty Defense:** If an external portal times out or blocks requests, the engine defaults to `ABSENT_UNKNOWN` (0 penalty points). The app never converts a network glitch into an unwarranted vendor penalty.

## 6. Machine Learning Diagnostics & Model Serving (Module C)

In addition to the deterministic compliance engine, the platform includes a trained **Calibrated Gradient-Boosted Forecaster with Scenario 4 Causal Signals** (LightGBM / CatBoost / XGBoost) to predict supplier self-resolution likelihood and calculate Expected Loss:

$$\text{Expected Loss} = \text{Invoice Tax} \times (1 - P_{\text{resolution}})$$

### Hierarchical Invariant Clamps (Deterministic Law > Black Box ML)
Under Indian GST jurisprudence under CGST Act Section 16(4) and Rule 48(4), tax credit cannot legally be claimed or held based solely on an opaque ML model. Therefore, our architecture implements a strict hierarchical dual-tier system:

1. **Tier 1: Statutory & Commercial Invariant Clamps (Primary & Overriding):**  
   - **Section 16(4) Calendar Expiry**: If `days_to_statutory_cutoff <= 0`, $P(\text{resolution})$ is hard-clamped to `0.0`.
   - **Rule 48(4) E-Invoicing Non-Compliance**: Mandated suppliers lacking an authenticated IRN on the portal are clamped to $P(\text{resolution}) \le 0.05$.
   - **Buyer-Posted Ledgers**: Buyer ERP entries without matching GSTR-1 credit are clamped to $P(\text{resolution}) \le 0.02$.
   - **Active Commercial Disputes**: Open rate disputes or unaccepted debit notes in buyer AP are clamped to $P(\text{resolution}) \le 0.10$.
2. **Tier 2: Calibrated GBDT Forecaster with Scenario 4 Causal Signals:**  
   Integrates point-in-time commercial and portal signals into tree ensembles, calibrated using Isotonic Regression fitted strictly on a dedicated calibration split.

### Model Benchmark on 1,000,000 Transactions

Evaluated under strict enterprise ML validation: **Vendor-Grouped Split** (Train 65% / Early-Stop 10% / Calibration 10% / Test 15%) across 2,000 unique supplier organizations on $N = 150,000$ unseen test transactions:

1. **Frontend on Vercel (`gst-credit-risk.vercel.app`):**

   Configured via `render.yaml` on the free tier with auto-deploy, environment secrets, and CORS allowance for Vercel and local origins.
3. **API & Demo Space on Hugging Face Spaces (`app.py`):**  
   Configured with the 100% free **Gradio SDK** in `app.py`. Mounts the full FastAPI backend onto Gradio Blocks via `gr.mount_gradio_app()`, satisfying HF space requirements while serving all `/api` endpoints and OpenAPI docs.
4. **Docker Container (`Dockerfile`):**  
   Multi-stage Python 3.12 slim container with pre-installed dependencies and pre-warmed model artifacts.
